LocalConnect

A marketplace platform connecting local business owners with student developers. Business owners post project requirements; developers discover projects and apply based on their skills. Separate roles for Business Owners, Developers and Admin.

Actively being developed — not a finished production application.

  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
  • Business & developer profiles, dashboards
  • Project posting, applications & status management
  • Centralized error handling middleware
  • REST API testing via Postman
